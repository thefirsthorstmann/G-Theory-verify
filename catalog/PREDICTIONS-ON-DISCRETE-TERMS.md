## 1 · Conduct

Four rules govern this registry, and they are the whole of its epistemology. **Stated status**: the values below are registered predictions of a research program; their constructions are internal to it and not presented here, and nothing in this document should be read as implying otherwise. **Maximal falsifiability**: every stake carries an exact value, a falsification band, a named adjudicating experiment, and a horizon; a claim that cannot fail has no place on this page. **No post-hoc wiggle**: the registry is timestamped in advance of the measurements; verdicts will be accepted as rendered, and no miss will be reinterpreted. **Portfolio, not lottery ticket**: the stakes are independent, the at-risk items are flagged before they resolve, the watched column shows what the program declines to bet, and the retired column shows what it has already lost and owned — the aggregate, not any single hit, is the evidence offered.

The precedent for registering numbers without mechanics is old and honorable: Hooke staked the spring law as an anagram in 1676; Balmer's formula ran twenty-eight years ahead of its mechanism; Koide's relation [4] has stood unexplained since 1981 and is tested respectfully to this day. The companion papers of this series [1, 2] argue the program's terms; this document only places its bets.

## 4 · At risk now

Two commitments stand closest to their adjudicators, and the registry flags them before the verdicts rather than after. **The proton charge-radius seat, 0.84131 fm**, sits +0.9σ from CODATA-2022's 0.84075(64); it fails outside 0.8398–0.8428 fm. **The electroweak pair's PDG-side posture** is exposure to the W-mass tension itself: a confirmed-high W mass convicts both members of stake 6 together. **The Higgs seat enters at risk on its face**: it sits 1.2σ below the PDG average and 2.3σ below the CMS value alone, and the coming precision decides it at many sigma either way. If any of these fails, this registry's next version will say so on its face.

## 6 · Retired, with reasons

A registry that hides its failures is not a registry. **A weak-mixing value in a running scheme** — retired as scheme-dependent, superseded the same day by the on-shell construction of stake 6. **A mass-ratio match at 391/140** — retired as chance-cheap: the match probability against the rational lattice was computed at roughly 41%, and a hit that cheap proves nothing. **A claimed 33.9 MeV splitting** — retired because it was never established as a prediction in the first place. Each retirement is preserved in the program's internal ledger with its arithmetic.
