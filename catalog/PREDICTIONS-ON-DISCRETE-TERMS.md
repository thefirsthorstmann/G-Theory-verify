## 1 · Conduct

Four rules govern this registry, and they are the whole of its epistemology. **Stated status**: the values below are registered predictions of a research program; their constructions are internal to it and not presented here, and nothing in this document should be read as implying otherwise. **Maximal falsifiability**: every commitment carries an exact value, a falsification band, a named adjudicating experiment, and a horizon; a claim that cannot fail has no place on this page. **No post-hoc wiggle**: the registry is timestamped in advance of the measurements; verdicts will be accepted as rendered, and no miss will be reinterpreted. **The whole record, not any single row**: the commitments are independent, the items in tension are flagged before they resolve, and the watched column shows what the program declines to commit — the aggregate, not any single agreement, is the evidence offered.

The precedent for registering numbers without mechanics is old and honorable: Hooke recorded the spring law as an anagram in 1676; Balmer's formula ran twenty-eight years ahead of its mechanism; Koide's relation [4] has stood unexplained since 1981 and is tested respectfully to this day. The companion papers of this series [1, 2] argue the program's terms; this document only records its commitments.

## 4 · In tension now

If any of these fails, this registry's next version will say so on its face.
