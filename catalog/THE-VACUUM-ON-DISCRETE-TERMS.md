## 1 · Introduction

The cosmological constant problem is the largest quantitative discrepancy
in physics. Summing the zero-point energies of the quantum fields over modes
up to the Planck scale gives a vacuum energy density some hundred and twenty
orders of magnitude above the value inferred from cosmological observation.
The discrepancy is not a small correction to be absorbed. It is a failure of
the calculation to produce anything near the measured quantity.

Several programmes treat this as a symptom rather than a computational
difficulty. Holographic approaches bound the vacuum energy by the area of a
cosmological horizon rather than by a volume mode sum, removing the
divergence by removing the sum. Unimodular formulations obtain the
cosmological term as a constant of integration rather than as a coupling in
the field equations, which changes what kind of quantity it is. Entropic and
information-theoretic treatments derive the field equations from horizon
degrees of freedom, so the vacuum term inherits a boundary origin rather
than a bulk one. What these approaches share is the position that the vacuum
energy is fixed by a boundary count and not by a mode sum.

This paper develops an account in that class, on a discrete register. The
proposal is that no mode sum exists to diverge. The register holds a finite
number of cells within any horizon, the vacuum term is the count of those
cells, and the hundred and twenty orders never arise because the quantity
that would produce them is not defined. The construction requires one
external quantity, an epoch, and §5 shows that it can require no fewer.

Five results are advanced, each labelled by its type. The vacuum's tier in
the mechanism is a theorem. The vacuum law and its fraction are an exact
identity, resting on one labelled identification and one established seat.
The observed excess is interpreted as a clock reading rather than a
constant. The classification of the remaining question is a second theorem.
A candidate dynamics is then proposed, carrying an exact crossing identity,
and is stated together with its standing against present data and the
measurements that would refute it. No parameter is adjusted at any point.

## 2 · The tier theorem: numerators source, the modulus idles

**Theorem 1.** On the wheel of the tethered prime, the k-th rider's closure falls
short by exactly k: k × 142857 × 7 = k·10⁶ − k, for every k from one to six, and
likewise for every full reptend prime. The modulus's own gap is one:
10⁶ − 999999 = 1 — for every rider, and with no rider at all.

The companion volume on gravitation builds its account on the first half: shortfall proportional to the
count carried is sourcing, and the equivalence principle and F ∝ M arrive from that
one congruence. The second half is this paper's object. **The modulus gap is
unsourced**: it does not scale with any rider, it belongs to the denominator rather
than the numerator, and it is present in the arithmetic whether or not anything
rides. It is the wheel's running cost — the unit shown
restarts every cycle. A skeptic will note the unit rider's shortfall is also one;
the two differ structurally: the rider's one grows to k when k rides, while the
modulus's one never grows at all. The distinction is scaling and locus, not magnitude.

**Interpretation 1.** The vacuum is the k-less tier: empty space is the wheel with
no numerator — an unridden wheel, which is empty of riders and not of structure.
The energy of empty space is the modulus gap read at the horizon.

**Interpretation 2 (the ladder join, labeled).** The companion's inverse square
theorem holds in the wheel's own extension count. Reading that count as the
horizon's count of Planck cells identifies two ladders — the arithmetic extension
and the physical boundary — and everything after this point stands on that join.
It is the identification that carries the paper, made once, here.

## 3 · The fraction: one unit per horizon

The seat of the dark energy fraction is established independently of this paper at
**two thirds** — the vacuum's rest at La, the same two thirds that anchors
the charged lepton mass relation. On the identification of Interpretation 2, the
vacuum law then takes a closed form with no adjustable element:

```
      ρ_Λ  ×  A / l_P²   =   ρ_P  ×  1

                    ⟺

                   Ω_Λ  =  2/3
```

because A/l_P² is 4πN², the horizon's full area in raw Planck cells.

**Theorem 2.** One Planck density in total, distributed over the horizon's raw
cell count, is exactly the statement Ω_Λ = 2/3: verified as an identity, for
arbitrary expansion rate and arbitrary gravitational constant, to twelve digits.
The Bekenstein quarter cell convention would give 8/3 and is excluded by the
seat: the raw cell is the convention the seat selects, and the selection is
recorded as the seat's, not as an a priori derivation.

The numerator deserves its own sentence. The one on the right side is the tier
theorem's object — the modulus gap, the unsourced unit, the reseed. **The vacuum
is one reseed per horizon**: the wheel's single unsourced unit, distributed over
the entire boundary count. The largest discrepancy between a theoretical prediction and observation — one hundred twenty
orders heavy — assumed the vacuum weighs something per cell. On discrete terms it
weighs one unit per horizon of cells: the correction comes from the counting
rather than from any cutoff.

One consequence of the seat is stated here because it is exact. In a flat
universe Ω_Λ = 2/3 implies a total matter fraction of one third — and on the
seat table one third is Fa's descending address, as two thirds is La's. In the figure's terms the cosmic fractions split on the thirds pair. The
unsourced sector rests at La. The sourced sector, meaning everything that
carries a k, stands at the seat of the lift, which is the agent of sourcing in
the companion volume. The reading of the split is the figure's; the arithmetic of the
complement is not.

## 4 · The excess is a clock reading

The observed fraction exceeds the seat: 0.6847 against 2/3 on the Planck-2018
fit — an excess of 2.7 percent — and 0.7025 on DESI's ΛCDM fit — an excess of
5.4. The excess is not a constant awaiting derivation. In the standard expansion
history the fraction sweeps, and on the Planck fit it crossed the seat at
redshift z\* = 0.028 — four hundred million years ago, now to within three
percent of cosmic history. The excess grows from zero at the crossing. Its rate is derived rather than
quoted. Differentiating the sweep and using Ω_Λ + Ω_m = 1 gives the rate at
the present epoch:

```
   dΩ_Λ/dz  =  −3 Ω_Λ Ω_m
```

The crossing redshift follows from the exact expansion history, by solving
Ω_Λ(z) = 2/3, and the measured excess is then compared against the rate
times that elapsed sweep:

| | Planck 2018 | DESI |
|---|---|---|
| Ω_Λ now | 0.6847 | 0.7025 |
| sweep rate | 0.648 | 0.627 |
| crossing z\* | 0.02782 | 0.05692 |
| **rate × z\*** (predicted) | **0.01802** | **0.03569** |
| **Ω_Λ − 2/3** (measured) | **0.01803** | **0.03583** |
| agreement | 0.10 % | 0.40 % |

It closes on **both** datasets to better than one per cent. The rate is not free. It is fixed by the same two
fractions the sweep is made of, so the closure is a check rather than a fit.
What it checks is that the sweep is linear across the interval: z* comes
from the exact expansion history, while the prediction is the linear
extrapolation.

**The excess is the crossing clock's reading**: the excess equals the elapsed time since the
crossing multiplied by the computed rate. A derivation of the excess's value
would therefore be a derivation of the elapsed time — of the present epoch —
and §5 proves the present epoch is the one quantity the theory must be
supplied externally.

The number of external epoch references a theory must be supplied is therefore

**"Now" is that reference.** The present epoch enters this paper exactly once —

the sector requires no second.

137 + 36/1000. The vacuum fraction's additive excess over its seat reads
18.0/1000 on the Planck fit and 35.8 ≈ 36/1000 on DESI's — the ledger, or the
ledger's double which is the fine-structure excess itself, dataset dependent. At today's uncertainty, ±7 in those units, any value lands near some

**The candidate's adjudication.** The same future data decide §6. Confirmation of negative running removes the
candidate together with ΛCDM. Resolution toward ΛCDM leaves the two separated

discrimination for the surveys after next.

## 8 · The correspondence: the vacuum term as a sustained tone

This section states the musical correspondence; nothing below depends on it.

Consider the structure of the vacuum from the point of view of a held
continuous tone — a drone. A drone is a tone that sounds under a melody
without belonging to it: the melody's phrases begin and end, and the drone
neither begins nor ends with them. Musical practice in many traditions
maintains such a tone deliberately, because melodic motion is heard as motion
relative to something that does not move.

Now read the tier theorem in those terms. The riders are the melody: each
phrase carries its own count k, each meets its own shortfall, and those
contributions are the sourced world — mass, pull. The modulus is the drone:
the wheel itself, sounding whether or not anything rides, its single unsourced
unit spread under the whole performance. Empty space is not silence on these
terms; it is the wheel turning with no numerator — the held tone under a
melody that happens to be absent. And the fraction of §3 states the drone's
level exactly: one unit — not one per cell, one per horizon.

The seat completes the reading. The vacuum's fraction rests at two thirds, and
two thirds is La's descending address — the fifth read downward, seated at 240
degrees on the figure. The vacuum rests, in the figure's own terms, at the
drone's interval.

## 9 · What would refute this

1. **The tier and the fraction.** Theorem 2 is exact. What is refutable is its application. A measured Ω_Λ
converging, at σ ≈ 0.002, away from both branches of the anatomy fork and
inconsistent with seat plus clock would leave the fraction's reading without
its excess accounted for.
2. **The equation of state.** The registered position is w = −1 exactly, with
   no fallback: a confirmed evolution refutes the account outright, and
   the registered schedule carries the null. Within that refutation's
   anatomy, a measured phantom crossing located away from the Ω_Λ = 2/3
   crossing removes §6's seat-geometry characterization specifically,
   since the model permits no other location; confirmation of negative
   running removes it jointly with ΛCDM.
3. **The epoch theorem.** A derivation of the present epoch from dimensionless
   structure — by anyone — would refute Theorem 3 as stated, exactly as a
   derivation of a magnitude from a pure number would refute its sibling.
4. **The sign.** The shortfall theorem permits no over
   closure, so the k-less term carries one sign: this framework's vacuum energy
   cannot be negative. A measured negative vacuum density would have refuted
   the mechanism outright. The observed density is positive.

## 10 · Relation to the standard account

One convention is named before the comparisons, because every comparison in
this paper runs through it. The observational quantities engaged here are the dark energy fraction, the
equation of state, and its running. None is a direct observable. They are
parameters of the standard cosmological model and its extensions, fitted to
the surveys' distance and clustering data. This paper engages the data through that parametrization, as every model in
the field does. Its statements about those parameters inherit it. That is why
the fraction is quoted on two fits separately rather than as one number. The background expansion throughout is the standard one, consistent
with the position that the field equations stand; what the paper
contributes is the density law's structure, not an alternative cosmography.

The holographic family of dark energy models is established literature, and
the dynamics of the event-horizon variant is known. This paper adds no new
mechanism to that family. It inherits the family's equations, and with them
its known conceptual tension: the present density is defined through the
future horizon, a
circularity the self-consistency of the flow exhibits but does not explain.
What this paper adds is one number. The family's free parameter is fixed here
at the seat, c_H² = 2/3, by the same structure that fixes the fraction, with
nothing fitted. That fixing produces the trinity, which fitted versions of the
model do not have. The cosmological constant problem's hundred and twenty
orders arise from weighing the vacuum per cell; the counting proposed here
weighs it once per horizon, and §3 is the entire correction. The coincidence problem is not solved here but relocated to a proven boundary,
the single epoch reference of Theorem 3. Two observations attach to it. The
present epoch sits within three percent of the seat crossing. In the candidate
model that crossing is also the phantom crossing and the horizon coincidence. The present observational situation is a preference of two to four sigma for
evolving dark energy with negative running. It presses on the registered null
directly: confirmed, it refutes the account, and it removes §6's
characterization together with ΛCDM. The account stands or falls with
named measurements rather than with argument.
