## 1 · Introduction: the two addresses of every crisis

List the principal pathologies of twentieth-century physics and note
where each sits. The ultraviolet catastrophe: an infinity of modes in a
finite box. The electron's classical self-energy: a divergence at zero
radius. The singularity theorems: curvature at infinity in finite time.
Renormalization: the subtraction of one infinity from another, performed
for half a century amid doubts about its legitimacy. Collapse of the
wavefunction: a change of state in zero time. Zeno: infinitely many
steps before any step completes. The list is not a coincidence of
unrelated troubles; it is one trouble in six forms. **Every crisis is a
physical claim staked at an end of the number line.** The position of
this paper, argued piecewise in the companion records [4, 5, 6] and
stated here as one doctrine, is that the ends of the number line are not
places. Zero and infinity are boundary markers of a bounded quantized
system: the names of edges, indispensable in notation, unoccupied in
fact.

## 2 · Definitions

The paper's objects are fixed before its arguments. A **bounded
register** is the pair (b, d): states are strings of at most d digits in
base b, and the **least count** is u = b⁻ᵈ — the smallest realized
difference. A **stage** is any finite configuration of a register; a
**realization** is a process that changes stages, one distinguishable
change at a time. The **doubling map** is D(x) = 2x and the **halving
map** its partial inverse; **octave equivalence** identifies x with 2x,
so that each nonzero quantity has a register index — *which* doubling —
and a class. The **completed limit** of an unbounded refinement is the
equivalence-class object analysis assigns to its tail; throughout, the
term names the notation's object, never a stage. On these definitions
the paper's claims are number theory before they are physics.

## 3 · Infinity: unperformable and uncertifiable

Two theorems from the companion papers carry the infinity half, and they divide the work cleanly between doing and knowing.

**A completed infinity cannot be performed.** The Navier–Stokes
companion [4] proved that finite-time singularity formation in a
turbulent cascade is a supertask, the completion of infinitely many
distinguishable physical operations in finite time. No finite-energy
system performs one: the operation supply is linear in time by the
Margolus–Levitin bound, while the cascade's demand is geometric in
depth, so the reachable depth is logarithmic in every resource. A cubic
meter of vigorously stirred water commands a budget sufficient for
forty-two cascade octaves; the singularity requires every octave there
is. The result binds any substrate with finite energy and local
interactions, molecular, field-theoretic or unknown, because it is a
theorem about distinguishable state change as such. **The notation's
infinity remains; no realization reaches it.**

**A completed limit cannot be certified.** The π companion [5] proved
the complementary epistemic fact. No finite set of finite-precision
measurements or computations distinguishes the completed infinite object
from a rational witness, and the witness is constructed explicitly at
every precision demanded. What finite processes possess of any limit is
exactly its brackets, its algorithms, and its samples. The completed
object is a convention of notation: one this paper uses freely, and one
that nothing observable has ever certified or could.

**An actual infinity has met measurement once.** History supplies the
third exhibit. Classical equipartition assigned the radiation field of a
finite cavity an actual infinity of participating modes, and for once
the commitment yielded a measurable consequence: divergent radiated
energy. Measurement contradicted it, and Planck's resolution was to
count [3]. The sole empirical engagement between an actual infinity and
an experiment ended with the infinite retired from the physics and the
count installed. It has never been re-admitted by evidence — only by
notation.

## 4 · Zero: the symmetric doctrine

The same argument runs to the other end of the line, where it is less
familiar. **What acts is never zero.** A mode of zero amplitude does
not sound; a process of zero energy performs no distinguishable change
— that is the Margolus–Levitin bound read at its floor, where the
orthogonalization time diverges; an interaction of zero duration
transmits nothing. Every realized quantity arrives with a least count:
one tick, one address, one distinguishable change. The zero of the
notation, exact, fixed and motionless, is therefore reached the way
infinity is reached: by writing, never by process. The companion
records name the calculable-but-unperformable values at this end *rest
values* [5]: exact objects that a system's realizations approach
without reaching, delivered by arithmetic alone. A measured zero is
always a bound, smaller than the instrument's least count. The
difference between "measured smaller than anything resolved" and "is
the exact zero" is precisely the difference Section 3 proved
undecidable by finite means, transposed down the line.

Division by zero completes the picture without mystery: it is not a forbidden operation on a real place but ungrammatical reference to a non-place — the notation's own marker refusing to be treated as an operand. A bounded quantized system never encounters the case.

## 5 · The ladder and the root: where the two infinities sit

The doubling ladder makes both ends of the line concrete, and number
theory disposes of them cleanly, in opposite ways. **Downward, the floor
is a theorem.** For every nonzero integer n, halving terminates: n =
2ᵛ·m with m odd and v finite, the 2-adic valuation. There is no infinite
descent toward zero inside the integers; the descent ends at an odd core
in finitely many steps, always. Zero is not approached from within the
system; it is postulated from without. **Upward, octave equivalence
reduces the infinite ladder to its root.** The ladder {2ⁿ} ascends
without bound, and under octave equivalence the entire two-sided ladder
is a *single class*: every 2ⁿ, for every n positive or negative, reduces
to the root, 1. This is the harmonic form of a very old maneuver. The
octave quotient does to the doubling ladder exactly what the projective
quotient does to parallel lines, adding a point and calling the family
convergent there, and what analysis does to a Cauchy tail. The
difference is that the bounded system *keeps the register index* — which
octave, which stage — the one datum every such quotient erases. Both
pins accompany the paper: the finite descent for every integer tested,
and the collapse of the whole ladder to the root class.

## 6 · What replaces the ends: the floor and the ceiling

Remove the two ends and nothing is lost but the crises; what remains is
a pair of rungs. **The floor is the least count**: beneath every
realized process sits the one tick, the one address, the single
distinguishable change — the unit that makes counting possible and zero
unoccupied. **The ceiling is the budget**: above every realized process
sits finite energy, hence finitely many operations, hence logarithmic
reach — the accounting that makes totals finite and infinity unoccupied.
Between floor and ceiling lies everything that happens. On these terms
the six forms of Section 1 reclassify together. Each crisis is a
commitment to an occupied end — actually infinitely many modes, an
actually zero radius, an actually completed limit — meeting a test that
only counting passes. The catastrophes were never discoveries about
nature; they were commitments to an ontology, and Section 3's exhibits
show that the commitment is never fulfilled.

Analysis itself emerges untouched, and the paper is at pains to say so.
The limit is a compression scheme of great power: a single symbol
summarizing an unbounded family of finite statements, with theorems
guaranteeing the summary's coherence. Physics' error was never the use
of the compression; it was mistaking the summary for the substrate,
reading the marker at the edge of the map as a place. Decompressed,
every limit statement physics needs is a statement about finite stages,
and those the bounded system supplies in any quantity — any finite
quantity.

## 7 · The shape of the edge: a cardioid drawn in thirds

The continuum's view of discrete doubling has an exact shape. Draw the
unit circle; join every angle t to its double 2t; let the chord family
accumulate. The chords envelope a curve, and the curve is classical: a
**cardioid**. The accompanying suite computes the envelope by
intersecting neighboring chords and finds its exact polar form. About
the axis point (−1/3, 0), the envelope satisfies r = (2/3)(1 + cos φ) to
numerical precision: *the cusp sits at minus one third, the scale is two
thirds*. The doubling map's fixed structure dictates the constants: the
map's two-cycle lives at the thirds of the circle, and the edge inherits
them. The reading, offered as a reading: the cardioid is the continuum's
image of the count. Where all stages of doubling are drawn at once, as
the notation draws them, the eye sees a single heart-shaped caustic with
one cusp. The discrete system underneath has no such curve; it has
chords, one per stage. The cardioid is what the completed family looks
like from the limit — exact, and belonging to the drawing rather than to
any stage of what is drawn.

## 8 · What is established, and what would refute it

Established: zero and infinity function in physics as boundary markers
of a bounded quantized system and are occupied by nothing physical. The
unperformability of completed infinities is proved in [4], the
uncertifiability of completed limits in [5], and the one historical test
is recorded in [3]. The finite-descent floor and the reduction of the
doubling ladder to its root are proved here in the accompanying pins,
and the cardioid is identified, exactly and in thirds, as the
continuum's image of the count. Analysis as mathematics is unchanged,
and every limit keeps its value. The distinction between potential and
actual infinity is Aristotle's [1], and finitism as a program is
Hilbert's [2]; the contribution here is the assembly, the two companion
theorems that support it, and the reclassification of the crisis list.
The doctrine is refuted by a physically realized completed infinite
process, which would refute the companion records as well, or by a
realized exact zero that acts: a process of strictly zero energy,
amplitude or duration with a distinguishable consequence. Either exhibit
ends the position; neither has ever been produced.

A condition that is met is recorded as a refutation and is not
reinterpreted. The program then uses the miss to find why the
construction failed, and a revised statement enters as a new one under
its own date.†

## 9 · Coda

The number line is drawn with two arrowheads because the drawing must
end somewhere and the numbers do not. The arrowheads mark directions,
not stations. A bounded quantized system lies between a floor it can
stand on and a ceiling it can count to, and everything physics has
measured lies there with it. The ends of the line belong to the
notation: indispensable, and empty.
