## 2 · How to read the sheet

Three of the rows are asymmetric in the program's favor and are flagged as
such. The equivalence-principle row is a standing prediction the framework
cannot adjust: any confirmed composition dependence, ever, at any precision,
removes the gravity mechanism outright, and each improvement of the null
deepens the commitment while tightening nothing. The sign row is already decided.
The G row cuts both ways, since convergence on the low group of
determinations near 6.6736 would be decisive corroboration at a precision
thirty-five times finer than the recommended uncertainty. The near-field rows are shape claims: the
scale λ₁ is unfixed, but the functional forms are parameter-free discriminators,
and a single detection at any range fixes the scale for every other prediction
at once. The last row is a commitment about future conduct rather than a present
claim: the Atom campaign registers its deviation-ledger predictions before any
fit, or it does not speak.

## 3 · What no measurement can decide

Stated so the boundary is as visible as the predictions. The dimensional boundary
itself — that no dimensionless construction yields a magnitude — is a theorem
about theories, tested by attempted counterexample rather than by instrument.
The choice of the wheel representation over the bare power is a statement about
where the derivation lives, thirty-nine orders below measurement, and is
defended as mathematics, not as physics. And the epoch reference — that "now"
enters once and cannot be derived — is refuted only by someone deriving the
present epoch from dimensionless structure, an invitation the vacuum paper
extends to every reader.
